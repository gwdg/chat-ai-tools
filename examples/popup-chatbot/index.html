Popup SAIA chatbot

This page embeds the same widget in popup mode. The chat stays out of the page layout and appears as an icon in the bottom-right corner. Click the icon to open it, and click it again to minimize it. Open the embedded example.

The popup keeps its conversation while minimized. It still shows the AI disclosure, the official EU “fully AI-generated” badge, and the Data privacy link.

Configure it with popup: true or the boolean popup attribute on <chat-ai-widget>.
